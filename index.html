01 The Story

A house built on light.

Maison Lumière began as a single candle in a forgotten cellar — a chef, a flame, and the conviction that dinner could be a memory you carry for years.

Today we sit at the centre of a restless town, yet inside time slows. Every plate is a chapter; every evening, a story told in courses.
